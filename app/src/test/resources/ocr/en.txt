Classic Pancakes

Serves 4

Ingredients

200 g plain flour

2 tsp baking powder
1 tbsp sugar

1 pinch of salt

2 eggs

300 ml milk

2 tbsp melted butter

Method

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large

bowl.

2. Whisk the eggs with the milk and butter, then pour into the

dry ingredients and stir until smooth.

3. Cook ladlefuls of batter in a hot pan for about two minutes

on each side.
